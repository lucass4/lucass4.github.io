## Writing standard

Applies to published prose, commit messages and pull request descriptions.

- Plain language. Say what a thing does, give the specific, stop.
- Shorter wins. Cut repeated explanations and background that does not help the reader.
- No "X rather than Y" antithesis. State the thing.
- No epigrams as labels or punchlines.
- No bold inline-header bullet lists. Prefer prose.
- No em-dashes. Use commas, periods, or restructure the sentence.
- No generic quality claims such as scalable, reliable or efficient. Name the mechanism.
- No "let me be honest" or "two things plainly" setups. Say the honest thing.
- Contractions are fine. State a fact once.

Verify before publishing. Every claim about what happened has to come from the repo, a
published case study, or something the author said in conversation. A plausible
reconstruction is an assumption, so ask instead of writing it in.

Prose wraps at about 90 columns, matching the existing content files.
