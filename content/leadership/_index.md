Building and running the DevOps Engineering function at Fortis Games, and the operating
models that came out of it. Each case study states what I decided, what I delegated, and
what I can't show.

Decisions get delegated to area leads, who get context and guardrails rather than answers.
Technical decisions get argued in writing and stay findable afterwards, through the RFC and
ADR process. The service ownership model names who's accountable for what platform owes
product teams and what they own themselves. The support rotation exists so no area stays
one person's private knowledge.
