Building and running the DevOps Engineering function at Fortis Games, and the operating
models that came out of it. Each case study states what I decided and what I delegated.

We were moving toward hands-on domain leads who could make decisions with context and
guardrails; that transition was not complete. Technical decisions were argued in writing
and stayed findable afterwards through the RFC and ADR process. The service ownership
model names who's accountable for what platform owes
product teams and what they own themselves. The support rotation exists so no area stays
one person's private knowledge.
